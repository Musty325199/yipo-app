# Yípo — Ajo & Esusu Record Keeper

Yípo is a fast, beautiful, and completely offline Android app built for Nigerian Ajo, Esusu, and Adashe rotating savings organizers to effortlessly track group members, contribution rounds, payout schedules, and turn swaps. Yípo functions strictly as a record-keeping ledger: it **only keeps records and never holds, touches, or moves money**. All group funds remain entirely between you and your trusted members in your regular bank accounts, while Yípo gives you crystal-clear accounting, automatic WhatsApp reminders, and professional statements.

---

## ✨ Features

- **Interactive Visual Ring**: Beautiful Skia-powered visual circle showing the active payout collector in the spotlight, member payment statuses (paid, part-paid, pending), and pot completion progress.
- **One-Tap Payment Recording**: Tap a member to instantly mark them paid for the round, with a 4-second instant undo toast. Tap again for part-payments, receipt attachments, and notes.
- **Share to Yípo**: Share payment receipts directly from WhatsApp or your phone gallery straight into Yípo to record contributions with full-screen receipt previews.
- **Direct WhatsApp Receipts & Reminders**: Send formatted contribution receipts and friendly, firm, or urgent reminders to members via WhatsApp with a single tap.
- **Fast Group Setup**: Paste entire member lists copied from WhatsApp chats with automatic name and number extraction, or pick members directly from your phone's address book.
- **Turn Swaps & Reliability Scoring**: Handle member turn swaps with recorded reasons and maintain a private, objective reliability rating for every member based on on-time contributions.
- **A4 PDF Statements**: Generate professional, print-ready A4 Ajo contribution statements with fully embedded offline typography.
- **Biometric Security**: Protect your group records with Fingerprint, Face ID, or Passcode app lock.
- **100% Offline & Private**: All data lives strictly in a local SQLite database on your device. No accounts, no external cloud servers, and no trackers.

## 📲 How to Install on Android

Follow these simple steps to install Yípo directly on your Android phone:

1. **Download the APK**: Download `yipo-v1.0.0.apk` from the [Releases](https://github.com/musty-dev01/yipo-app/releases) page.
2. **Open the File**: Open your phone's **Downloads** folder or tap the download notification, then tap the `.apk` file.
3. **Allow Installation from Unknown Sources**:
   - If Android displays a popup saying *"For your security, your phone is not allowed to install unknown apps from this source"*, tap **Settings**.
   - Turn on the toggle next to **"Allow from this source"** (for Chrome, Files, or WhatsApp).
   - Press the back button and tap **Install**.
4. **Play Protect Warning**:
   - If Google Play Protect shows a dialog saying *"Unrecognized app"* or *"Blocked by Play Protect"*, tap **"More details"** (or the dropdown arrow).
   - Tap **"Install anyway"**.
   - *(Note: This standard Android alert appears for any app installed directly from a download link outside the Google Play Store.)*
5. **Open Yípo**: Once installed, tap **Open** to launch the app and set up your first Ajo group!

## 💾 Back Up Your Records

Because Yípo respects your privacy and stores all records locally on your phone, you are in full control of your data.

To create a backup:
1. Open Yípo and tap the **Settings** tab.
2. Under **Data & Storage**, tap **Export Backup**.
3. Save the generated `.json` backup file to your Google Drive, email it to yourself, or send it to your personal WhatsApp chat.

You can import this file anytime via **Settings > Import Backup** on any Android device to restore all your groups, members, payment histories, and notes.
